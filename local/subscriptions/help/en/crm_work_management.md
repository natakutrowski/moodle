# Work Management

## What is a Work Item?

Work Items are the internal work management system of CampusFR.

They are used to organize:

- support requests
- technical incidents
- bugs
- content production
- marketing actions
- administrative tasks
- customer follow-up

Each Work Item has its own lifecycle and may be linked to users, Inbox conversations, automations or other CRM objects.

---

# Inbox vs Work Items

Inbox stores conversations.

Work Items store the actual work that must be completed.

A conversation may generate no Work Item.

A single conversation may also generate several Work Items.

---

# Lifecycle

Available statuses:

- Open
- In Progress
- Waiting
- Blocked
- Resolved
- Closed
- Cancelled

Every status transition is recorded in the history.

---

# Priorities

- Low
- Normal
- High
- Urgent
- Critical

Urgent Work Items appear in operational dashboards.

---

# Teams

Work Items may be assigned:

- directly to a team member;
- to an entire team.

This simplifies workload distribution.

---

# Subtasks

A Work Item may contain several subtasks.

This allows complex requests to be divided into smaller actions.

---

# Internal comments

Comments are visible only to internal staff.

They are never sent to customers.

---

# CRM links

A Work Item may be linked to:

- Moodle users
- Inbox conversations
- CRM contacts
- subscriptions
- payments
- automation rules
- Intelligence alerts

---

# Best practices

Create one Work Item per business objective.

Use internal comments to document decisions.

Close a Work Item only when every required action has been completed.
