# Discover the CampusFR CRM

The CampusFR CRM centralizes the tools required to manage users, subscriptions and digital purchases.

## Dashboard

The Dashboard displays the most important current information:

- new users;
- new subscriptions;
- digital revenue;
- at-risk profiles;
- hot leads;
- commercial opportunities;
- items requiring attention.

You can display indicators for today, the current week or the current month.

## Command Center

The Command Center lets you quickly search for:

- a user;
- a subscription;
- a digital purchase;
- a product;
- an administrative action.

Press `Ctrl + K` or `Cmd + K` to open it.

## User profiles

Each user profile brings together:

- personal information;
- subscriptions;
- purchases;
- tags;
- notes;
- timeline;
- CRM scores and recommendations.

## CRM Intelligence

The Intelligence engine analyses profiles and detects:

- hot leads;
- at-risk users;
- VIP customers;
- upgrade opportunities;
- inactive users.

> CRM Intelligence helps administrators make decisions. It does not replace human validation.
