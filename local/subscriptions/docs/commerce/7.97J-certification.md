# Commerce 7.97J — certification

7.97J certifies the pedagogical promotion architecture delivered by 7.97A–I.

Certification invariants:

- legacy/classic course access is not restricted;
- promotion access is individual, never a course-global Moodle restriction;
- a three-lesson DEV course is sufficient to certify calendar behavior;
- multiple Commerce products/formulas may target one pedagogical promotion;
- RU/FR offer variants may share the same course and promotion;
- groups remain optional and promotion-specific;
- completed progressive calendars become lifetime-full access;
- closed/full sale policies are enforced before purchase and rechecked after payment;
- full refund/cancellation can terminate pedagogical participation and group membership;
- commercial discount promotions remain a distinct domain from pedagogical promotions;
- 7.97 lifecycle history is additive: progressive relations created before the lifecycle ledger
  keep their calendar semantics until explicit lifecycle history exists.

Manual UX/polish is intentionally outside this technical certification pass.
